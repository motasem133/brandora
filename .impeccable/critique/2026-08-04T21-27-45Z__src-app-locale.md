## Regressions this pass introduced, and fixed

Three of the five most serious findings were mine, from this same batch of work:

- **[P0] The wizard accepted one character per click.** An inline `ref={(node) => node?.focus()}` re-attaches on every render, and the panel re-renders on every keystroke — typing "Acme" left "A". The e2e suite missed it because `.fill()` sets a value in one operation. Fixed with an effect keyed on the step, and a test that types.
- **[P0] Every confirmation dialog announced the same action.** `ConfirmDialog` hard-coded `id="confirm-title"`; billing renders three at once, so all three pointed `aria-labelledby` at the first. Opening "Change plan" announced "Cancel your subscription?". Fixed with `useId` — verified: the three dialogs now name Cancel, Switch to Basic, Switch to Business.
- **Hydration mismatch on the builder** from `typeof window` in the publish URL. Resolved in the click handler instead.
- **The reduced-motion substitute was a dead selector** — aimed at `pulse-ring`, the keyframe, while the markup carries `pulse-brand`. Half the fix I reported as done was doing nothing.
- **"Most chosen"** on the Pro plan is a usage claim PRODUCT.md forbids outright. Now "Recommended".

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The published customer site puts unchecked generated colour under text.** `block-renderer.tsx` and `sites/[subdomain]` never import `brand-theme.ts`. Measured: a light-primary palette renders a CTA label at **1.42:1** and a contact email at **1.39:1**; the product's own demo palette renders the footer at 4.30:1. The machinery to fix it exists, is provably correct, and is used everywhere except the one surface the customer publishes under their client's name.

**[P1] Billing kept both banned patterns while every neighbour dropped them.** Three same-size stat cards, four same-size plan cards, six gradient fills in one viewport. It was the page the last critique named, and the shape now shipping on admin is exactly what it proposed.

**[P1] No skip link on any of 22 routes, unchanged.** 10 chrome tab stops before content on every signed-in route, the first two sharing the accessible name "Overview".

**[P1] Selection by colour alone in the wizard.** `OptionButton` and `YesNo` carry no `aria-pressed` — while the colour swatches in the same file do, three hundred lines away.

**[P2] Status colours have no light-theme value.** `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger` are declared once and never overridden, so every badge and every `+490` measures 2.00–3.76:1 in light. Both B and C found this independently.

**[P2] `Eyebrow` has zero adopters** while its exact hand-rolled string survives in 9 places. The same failure mode as the previous pass's `Textarea` — a primitive written as the answer to a measured drift, then not wired in.

## What's Working

- **The radius lint rule** turned a 56-vs-43 loss into 123-vs-6, mechanically, with correctly reasoned exemptions for the surfaces that render a customer's site.
- **Both modals are native `<dialog>` with `showModal()`**, and the dialog title deliberately is not a heading so it stays out of the page outline.
- **RTL is the strongest dimension in the codebase**: 37 logical utilities against one direction-neutral physical pair, and zero overflow at 320px in both directions.
- **Reduced motion substitutes rather than deletes** — transforms and filters stripped, colour and opacity kept at 120ms, and the two states that exist only as movement given a still form.
